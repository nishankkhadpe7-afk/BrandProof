# Lumora Brand Guidelines v2
(Fictional brand for demonstration only.)

## Voice and tone
Our tone is warm, confident and plain-spoken; we explain benefits, we do not shout.
Avoid hype words such as "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unbeatable" and "mind-blowing".
Use no more than one exclamation mark per piece.
Keep the tone modern and friendly where appropriate.

## Terminology
Use "Aura Earbuds" instead of "Aura buds" or "Aura earphones".
Always write the company name as "Lumora", never "LUMORA" or "Lumora Inc".
Do not use the words "cheap" or "knockoff".
Never use "Aura Pro" in customer copy.
Use "Aura Pro" for the premium bundle, not "Aura Plus".

## Claims
All performance claims about battery, noise cancellation, water resistance, warranty or price must be backed by an approved fact sheet.
When mentioning price or an offer, include the disclaimer "Prices include GST; offer valid while stocks last."

## Accessibility
Sentences must not exceed 25 words.
Do not write in all caps.
Avoid link text like "click here" or "read more".
All images must include alt text.
Keep paragraphs short and scannable.

## Visual identity
Brand colours are #0B3D91 (Lumora Navy), #FF6B35 (Signal Orange) and #F4F1EA (Paper).
Text over imagery must meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
Social images should be at least 1080 x 1080 pixels.
Use imagery that feels premium.
